\begin{abstract}
We develop the mathematical core of the Recognition Null Kernel Engine (RNKE), a verification architecture for formalizable systems in which claims, evidence, dependencies, admissible transitions, and unresolved obligations are represented explicitly and a candidate update may be committed only after independently specified admissibility conditions close. The current public mathematical challenge is a deliberately narrow benchmark of this architecture: it tests whether a formal conclusion can be promoted when its proof flow, numerical enclosure, dependency structure, or remainder obligations do not actually justify it. The finite core begins from a structured initial datum with no accepted claims, introduces an invariant-preserving transition law, separates candidates from validators, detects target-changing blind directions, and derives exact target-support, curvature, proof-admission, threshold, fixed-state, status, refinement, and replay-persistence conditions. We then give a stronger finite-to-infinite extension whose Hilbert carrier is obtained by quotienting and completing a declared positive form rather than introduced as an unexplained primitive. An involution-graded generator produces a bilateral exponential flow and a nested observer-jet tower with an exact remainder. Corrected finite packets are transported to one common carrier and completed in operator norm with explicit Gram-error bounds. A vanishing faithfulness residual forces the limiting adverse memory into a finite observer range; a variational theorem derives the minimum faithful observer rank from that range. Only after these obligations close is the limiting sign reduced to a finite Hermitian matrix, whose eigenvalues carry the negative index, kernel, and normalized positivity margin of the infinite signed form. A finite outward matrix bound plus a proved completion error therefore yields a strict limiting certificate. Finite certification is tied to finite target-relevant obstruction rank and verified completion, not to finite ambient dimension, untracked truncation, or assertion alone.
\end{abstract}
