\subsection{Representation covariance of the finite certificate}

\begin{corollary}[Unitary covariance]
In the finite-channel Hilbert compression corollary, let $U:\mathcal H\to\mathcal H$ and $Q:\C^m\to\C^m$ be unitary. Define
\[
S'=USU^*,
\qquad
V'=UVQ.
\]
If $C$ is the minimum source lift of $V$, then the minimum source lift of $V'$ is
\[
C'=UCQ,
\]
and the corresponding Hermitian compression is
\begin{equation}
B_C'=(C')^*C'=Q^*B_CQ.
\label{eq:compression-covariance}
\end{equation}
Consequently the spectrum of $B_C$, its largest eigenvalue, its threshold multiplicities, and every decision depending only on its unitary similarity class are independent of the ambient Hilbert-space representation and of the orthonormal channel labels.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Functional calculus gives $(S')^{1/2}=US^{1/2}U^*$. Hence
\[
(S')^{1/2}C'
=US^{1/2}U^*UCQ
=US^{1/2}CQ
=UVQ
=V'.
\]
Moreover $UCQ$ takes values in $(\ker S')^\perp=U(\ker S)^\perp$, so uniqueness of the minimum source lift gives $C'=UCQ$. The identity \cref{eq:compression-covariance} follows immediately.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This covariance is why the terminal finite Hermitian matrix is a certificate of the target-relevant obstruction rather than an artifact of one coordinate chart. If the variational observer theorem yields rank five, the certificate remains five-dimensional under every unitary change of the completed carrier, but no fixed matrix size is asserted when the target-relevant memory rank changes.
\end{remark}
